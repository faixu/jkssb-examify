Indian Freedom Struggle (1857 - 1950) - Statement-Based MCQ
1. Consider the following statements regarding the Revolt of 1857:
1. The revolt was primarily sparked by the introduction of the Enfield rifle.
2. The educated middle class of India actively supported the revolt.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The educated middle class largely remained aloof or opposed the revolt, believing that
British rule would help modernize India.
2. Consider the following statements about the Indian National Congress (INC):
1. The first session of the INC was held in Bombay in 1885.
2. A.O. Hume was the first President of the INC.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The first session was held in Bombay in 1885, but Womesh Chandra Bonnerjee was its
first President, not A.O. Hume (who was the founder/general secretary).
3. Consider the following statements regarding the Swadeshi Movement:
1. It was launched as a reaction to the Partition of Bengal in 1905.
2. It was completely restricted to the province of Bengal and did not spread elsewhere.

-- 1 of 105 --

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The movement started in Bengal but soon spread to other parts of the country like Poona,
Bombay, Madras, and Punjab.
4. Consider the following statements regarding the Surat Split of 1907:
1. It resulted in the division of the INC into Moderates and Extremists.
2. The Extremists wanted Rash Behari Ghosh to be the president of the Surat session.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The Extremists wanted Lala Lajpat Rai or Bal Gangadhar Tilak as the president, while the
Moderates supported Rash Behari Ghosh.
5. Consider the following statements regarding the Morley-Minto Reforms (1909):
1. It introduced the system of separate electorates for Muslims.
2. It allowed Indians to become members of the Viceroy’s Executive Council for the first time.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: C
Explanation: Both statements are correct. Satyendra Prasad Sinha became the first Indian to join the
Viceroy's Executive Council.
6. Consider the following statements regarding the Ghadar Party:
1. It was founded in San Francisco, USA.
2. Its primary aim was to achieve independence through constitutional means.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The Ghadar Party was a revolutionary group that aimed to overthrow British rule in India
through armed rebellion, not constitutional means.
7. Consider the following statements about the Home Rule League Movement:
1. Annie Besant launched her Home Rule League before Bal Gangadhar Tilak.
2. The movement aimed to achieve self-government for India within the British Empire.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: B
Explanation: Tilak launched his Home Rule League in April 1916, while Annie Besant launched hers in
September 1916.
8. Consider the following statements regarding the Lucknow Pact (1916):
1. It brought the Moderates and Extremists back together within the INC.

-- 3 of 105 --

2. The INC rejected the Muslim League's demand for separate electorates during this session.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: During the Lucknow Pact, the INC actually accepted the Muslim League's demand for
separate electorates, a major step in Hindu-Muslim unity at the time.
9. Consider the following statements regarding the Champaran Satyagraha (1917):
1. It was Mahatma Gandhi’s first major political involvement in India.
2. It was organized against the oppressive Ryotwari system.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: It was organized against the oppressive Tinkathia system, where European planters
forced peasants to grow indigo on 3/20th of their land.
10. Consider the following statements regarding the Rowlatt Act (1919):
1. It allowed the government to imprison any person without trial and conviction in a court of law.
2. The Jallianwala Bagh massacre occurred during protests against this Act.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2

-- 4 of 105 --

D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: C
Explanation: Both statements are correct. The Act gave sweeping powers to the government, leading to
widespread protests.
11. Consider the following statements regarding the Non-Cooperation Movement:
1. It was launched simultaneously with the Khilafat Movement.
2. The movement was called off by Gandhi following the Chauri Chaura incident.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: C
Explanation: Gandhi combined the Non-Cooperation movement with the Khilafat issue to foster Hindu-
Muslim unity, and abruptly halted it after violence erupted in Chauri Chaura in 1922.
12. Consider the following statements regarding the Swaraj Party:
1. It was founded by Motilal Nehru and C.R. Das.
2. Its main objective was to boycott the legislative councils.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The Swaraj Party's objective was to enter the legislative councils and wreck the
government from within, contrary to the "No-Changers" who wanted to boycott the councils.
13. Consider the following statements regarding the Simon Commission:

A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: It was boycotted by major Indian political parties because it did not have a single Indian
member. It also did not promise Dominion Status at the time.
14. Consider the following statements regarding the Nehru Report (1928):

2. It demanded complete independence (Purna Swaraj) for India.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The Nehru Report demanded Dominion Status for India, not complete independence.
Purna Swaraj was demanded later at the Lahore session in 1929.

Swaraj (Complete Independence).
16. Consider the following statements regarding the Civil Disobedience Movement:

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

B) 2 only

Answer: C
Explanation: Both statements are correct. Gandhi's march to Dandi to break the salt monopoly kicked
off the movement.
17. Consider the following statements regarding the Round Table Conferences:

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

B) 2 only

D) Neither 1 nor 2

Gandhi). Dr. B.R. Ambedkar attended all three.

18. Consider the following statements regarding the Gandhi-Irwin Pact (1931):
1. The British government agreed to release all political prisoners, including those convicted of violence.
2. The INC agreed to suspend the Civil Disobedience Movement.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: B
Explanation: The British agreed to release only those political prisoners who were not convicted of
violence (which is why Bhagat Singh and his comrades were not released).
19. Consider the following statements regarding the Communal Award (1932):
1. It was announced by British Prime Minister Winston Churchill.
2. It extended the system of separate electorates to the Depressed Classes.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: B
Explanation: It was announced by British Prime Minister Ramsay MacDonald. It extended separate
electorates to the Depressed Classes, which Gandhi strongly opposed.
20. Consider the following statements regarding the Poona Pact:
1. It was signed between Mahatma Gandhi and Lord Irwin.
2. It resulted in the abandonment of separate electorates for the Depressed Classes in favor of reserved
seats in joint electorates.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

-- 8 of 105 --

A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: B
Explanation: It was signed between Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and leaders representing Gandhi (who was
fasting in jail). It replaced separate electorates with reserved seats.
21. Consider the following statements regarding the Government of India Act 1935:
1. It provided for the establishment of an All-India Federation.
2. It abolished provincial autonomy and introduced dyarchy at the provinces.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The Act actually abolished dyarchy at the provincial level and introduced provincial
autonomy. It proposed dyarchy at the center.

B) 2 only

D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A

23. Consider the following statements regarding the Cripps Mission (1942):

A) 1 only
B) 2 only

Explanation: The Cripps Mission offered Dominion Status, not complete independence. This was a
major reason for its rejection.
24. Consider the following statements regarding the Quit India Movement (1942):

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: B
Explanation: It was launched in response to the failure of the Cripps Mission (not the Cabinet Mission,
which came in 1946). Parallel governments were established in places like Ballia and Satara.

26. Consider the following statements regarding the Cabinet Mission (1946):

2. It proposed a weak central government with strong, autonomous provinces.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: B
Explanation: The Cabinet Mission rejected the demand for a separate state of Pakistan and instead
proposed a three-tier federal structure with a weak center.
27. Consider the following statements regarding the Mountbatten Plan (1947):

A) 1 only
B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2

-- 11 of 105 --

D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The plan proposed partition but established the Radcliffe Boundary Commission to draw
the actual borders between the two nations.
28. Consider the following statements regarding the Indian Independence Act 1947:

B) 2 only
C) Both 1 and 2
D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: C
Explanation: Both statements are correct. The princely states were freed from British paramountcy, and
the assemblies gained full legislative powers.

Explanation: Hyderabad was integrated through police action (Operation Polo). Junagadh was
integrated through a plebiscite.
